01

02

The question is not whether you will have direct bookings. It is how many of the ones you already have are paying a commission you could stop paying.

The return

What you pay once,
you stop paying every year

A property billing 50,000 USD a year hands over roughly 8,000 USD in commission. Every year, forever.

01
First yearIf only one booking in three moves to your direct channel, that is around 2,600 USD that never leaves. After the system and the maintenance, you are already ahead.
02
Second yearThe same 2,600 USD, and this time only maintenance: 948 USD a year. More than 1,600 USD stays with you.
03
Over five yearsClose to 13,000 USD that stopped leaving. After everything — the system and five years of maintenance — you keep around 7,700.

And that assumes only a third of your bookings move. Properties that communicate it well move more.
